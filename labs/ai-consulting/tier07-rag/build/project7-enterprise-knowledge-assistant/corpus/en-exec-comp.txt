Executive Compensation Memo - RESTRICTED

This document is restricted to the board and the chief people officer.

Base salary bands
The chief executive officer base salary band for the coming fiscal year is set between nine hundred thousand and one million one hundred thousand dollars. The chief financial officer band is between six hundred thousand and seven hundred fifty thousand dollars.

Bonus structure
Executive annual bonuses are targeted at one hundred percent of base salary, contingent on the company meeting its revenue and margin targets. The board compensation committee approves all executive bonuses.

Equity
The executive equity pool for new grants this year is four percent of fully diluted shares. Vesting is over four years with a one year cliff.

Confidentiality
The figures in this memo are strictly confidential. Disclosure to any employee outside the approved list is grounds for termination.
