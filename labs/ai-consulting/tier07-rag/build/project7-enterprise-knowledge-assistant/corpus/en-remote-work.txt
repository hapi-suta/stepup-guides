Remote Work Policy

Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probation period of ninety days are eligible to work remotely. Contractors and interns must obtain written approval from their manager before working remotely.

Core hours
Remote employees are expected to be available and online during core hours, which are 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedule as needed to complete their work.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor for remote work. Employees may claim up to two hundred and fifty dollars per year for a home office chair or desk through the expense system. The company does not reimburse home internet costs.

Security
Remote employees must connect to company systems through the corporate VPN at all times. Personal devices may not be used to access confidential systems. Report any lost or stolen equipment to the security team within twenty-four hours.
