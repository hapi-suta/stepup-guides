Expense Reimbursement Policy

Submitting expenses
Employees must submit expense reports within thirty days of incurring the expense. Reports submitted after thirty days may be rejected. Every expense over twenty-five dollars requires an itemized receipt.

Travel
Economy class airfare is reimbursed for all flights under six hours. Business class may be booked for flights over six hours with prior manager approval. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to two hundred dollars per night in standard cities and three hundred dollars per night in high-cost cities.

Meals
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to seventy-five dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment meals require the names of the attendees and the business purpose to be recorded.

Approval
Expense reports are approved by the employee's direct manager. Reports over five thousand dollars additionally require finance department approval.
