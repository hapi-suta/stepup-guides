# AI Use-Case Intake Form

**Purpose:** the single front door for proposing any new AI use case at
[ORGANIZATION]. Anyone may submit. Submission triggers a risk classification and
the AI project approval workflow. No AI system is built or bought without one.

**Aligns to:** NIST AI RMF (Map), ISO/IEC 42001 (planning).

> Fill in every field. "I don't know yet" is an acceptable answer - it tells the
> reviewer where the risk is. Do not leave fields blank.

## Section B - The problem and the benefit

1. **What business problem does this solve?** State it in money, time, or risk
   terms, not model terms. [ANSWER]
2. **What is the expected benefit, and how would we measure it?** [ANSWER]
3. **What is the current way this is done (the baseline)?** [ANSWER]
4. **Why is AI the right tool here, rather than a simple rule or existing
   software?** [ANSWER]

## Section C - People affected

5. **Who is directly affected by this system?** (e.g. applicants, customers,
   staff) [ANSWER]
6. **Who is indirectly affected?** [ANSWER]
7. **Does this system make or influence a decision about a person's money,
   health, freedom, employment, or access to a service?** (Yes/No) [ANSWER]
   *(A "Yes" strongly suggests high-risk - see the risk-classification matrix.)*

## Section D - Data

8. **What data does it use, and where does that data live today?** [ANSWER]
9. **Does it use personal or confidential data?** (Yes/No, which categories)
   [ANSWER]
10. **Do we have the rights/consent to use this data for this purpose?** [ANSWER]

## Section E - Vendors and build-vs-buy

11. **Would this be built in-house, bought from a vendor, or both?** [ANSWER]
12. **If a vendor, which one(s) are being considered?** [ANSWER]
    *(A vendor requires a vendor questionnaire before approval.)*

## Section F - Oversight and harm (first pass)

13. **What is the worst thing that happens if the system is wrong?** [ANSWER]
14. **Who would review or override its output?** [ANSWER]
15. **How would an affected person complain or appeal a decision?** [ANSWER]
