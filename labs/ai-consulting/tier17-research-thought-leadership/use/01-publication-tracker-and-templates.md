# USE: Publication Tracker and Artifact Templates

**Tier 17 - USE phase.** This exercise gives you the tracker that holds you to the publication target, plus fill-in templates for the highest-value artifact types. Copy these into your `~/thought-leadership/` repository and use them for real. Placeholders: `[CLIENT]`, `[COMPANY]`, `[INDUSTRY]`.

**Validated on:** editorial review, 2026-07-25.

---

## The publication target (what you are tracking)

By the end of the path:
- 12 strong technical articles
- 4 executive AI briefs
- 3 published case studies
- 2 conference presentations
- 1 peer-reviewed or doctoral research publication
- 1 open-source AI governance toolkit
- 1 major public-sector AI demonstration

## Template: Technical article

```
# [TITLE - specific, not clickbait]

## The problem
[One concrete paragraph. A real situation, not "AI is important".]

## The claim
[One or two sentences. Specific and falsifiable.]

## The evidence
[From your work, data, or cited sources. Every citation real and verifiable.]

## The method
[What a reader can reuse. Steps or a framework.]

## Where this does not apply
[Honest limits. This paragraph builds trust.]

## Takeaway
[The one sentence to remember.]
```

## Template: Executive AI brief

```
# Executive Brief: [TOPIC]
For: [AUDIENCE, e.g. public-sector leadership]   Length: 1-2 pages

## The bottom line (read this if nothing else)

## The options
| Option | Upside | Downside | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
## Recommendation
## Risks and how we manage them
## What we need from you
```

## Template: Case study

```
# Case Study: [ANONYMIZED TITLE]
Permission obtained: [yes/date]   Identifying detail removed: [confirmed]

## Context
[Anonymized client type, sector, and the problem. No identifying detail.]

## The approach
[The method you applied - the reusable part.]

## What happened
[Results, honestly. Include what did not work and what you would change.]

## Lessons
[What a reader can take to their own situation.]

## Reusable method
[Distilled steps others can follow.]
```

2. Fill the tracker with your planned pieces and update it every week.
3. Before publishing anything, run the pre-publish check (next file).
