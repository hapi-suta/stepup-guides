# Not injectable: 06-disk-full-during-backup

The fault is a full filesystem under the pgBackRest repository (`/opt/pgsql/backup`). On a single lab node that directory lives on the root filesystem, so filling it would drive free space below the 1 GB safety floor and could take down sshd, the tutor engine and PostgreSQL itself; mounting a small loop device over `/opt/pgsql/backup/pgbackrest` would hide the learner's real repository and change what the fix path means. Do not fake it. The tutor should walk the runbook from pasted output instead: ask the learner to paste `df -h` and `sudo du -sh /opt/pgsql/backup/pgbackrest/*`, have them explain which files are safe to remove (fill file, expired backups via `pgbackrest expire`, old logs older than 7 days), then ask for the `pgbackrest --stanza=main info` and `check` output after the retry to confirm a new full backup exists.
